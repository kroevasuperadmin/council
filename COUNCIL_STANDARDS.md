# Council review standard

Judge the actual rendered work, not the creator's intention. A deck is a pass only when a person at the back of a large room can understand it in seconds.

## Non-negotiables

- One slide, one idea. Keep the headline to one clear claim and use short supporting copy. Delete rather than shrink.
- Huge-screen readability: title at least 32 pt, body at least 20 pt, labels at least 14 pt. Reject small, faint, crowded, clipped, overlapping or low-contrast text. Check the rendered slide, not only the source.
- Use high contrast: dark ink on Dossair paper or near-white text on dark background. Pale grey on white, grey on teal, and thin text are failures.
- Dossair is the star in external decks. Use its paper `#F5F6F3`, ink `#10191E`, registry teal `#0E5A61`, fine rules, Newsreader/Inter/IBM Plex Mono editorial system. Rizqey Labs' black/indigo system is only correct when the work is explicitly for Rizqey Labs.
- The title, logo, URL and call to action must name Dossair where Dossair is the product. Do not substitute Rizqey Labs branding.
- Write in Faris's plain, direct voice: familiar words, specific claims, no jargon, no puffery, no em dashes. Do not invent metrics, customers, rankings, partnerships, quotes or outcomes. Mark an unsupported claim `[CONFIRM]`.
- Check rhythm and geometry: no accidental white gaps in a phrase, uneven word spacing, orphaned elements, stretched logos, collisions or empty space that makes the slide look broken.
- Each slide must earn its space. The ITEC Top 30, AltaLab and Zemyth work won attention by making one credible point at a time, using restrained editorial layouts, clear evidence, and a founder-readable story instead of a wall of AI copy.

## Required review output

For every supplied image, say what you can actually see. List each failure with the slide/page, severity, why it fails this standard, and an exact fix. If images were not supplied, abstain from visual judgement; do not guess. Prioritise a defect that would embarrass Faris in a live room over minor wording polish.

## What a fix looks like

1. Replace a 14 pt paragraph with a 28 pt, two-line statement; move detail to speaker notes or the next slide.
2. Replace grey `#888` body text on white with `#10191E` ink or remove it.
3. Close a visible split between `We` and a coloured `win` so the title reads as one phrase.
4. Replace a Rizqey Labs black/indigo cover with Dossair paper, teal accent, Dossair name and mark.
5. Replace “AI-powered application workflow orchestration” with “Dossair fills grant applications with you.”
6. Replace “10x faster” with a measured, sourced result, or `[CONFIRM]` until evidence exists.
7. Turn five competing cards into one headline, one proof point and one next step.
8. Replace a stretched or recoloured logo with the supplied, proportionate brand asset.
9. Turn “Our synergistic platform -- built for scale” into “We help founders finish applications.”
10. Move a CTA lost in a dense footer into one large, high-contrast line: “Start your first application.”
