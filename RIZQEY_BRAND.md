# Rizqey brand sheet

Council must grade visual quality, branding and layout, not only wording.

## Source of truth

This sheet reflects the live sites and supplied screenshots reviewed 9 October 2026. Do not use earlier cream-background or green-accent guidance.

## Rizqey Labs

### Colour

- Canvas: `#0A0A0A`.
- Surfaces: `#111111` and `#161616`.
- Primary text: `#F0F0F0`; muted text: `#888888`.
- Accent: indigo `#5461C8`. Use a restrained dim tint of `rgba(84,97,200,.1)` when a surface needs separation.
- Hairlines: `rgba(255,255,255,.07)`.

### Type and logo

- Display: DM Serif Display, regular 400. Use italic indigo for the key phrase or word. Fallback: Georgia, serif.
- Body and navigation: Inter, 400 to 600. Fallback: Arial, sans-serif.
- Use `C:/Users/diony/dev/rizqey/logo-dark.png` on dark backgrounds. Do not recolour, crop, stretch, or recreate it.

### Composition

Rizqey Labs is dark and editorial: wide margins, small uppercase labels, thin rules, one serif statement, then quiet supporting copy. The reference line is: “The work gets done. *You say yes before anything leaves.*” Keep indigo sparse. Avoid a dashboard, card grid, warm canvas, or green accent.

## Dossair

Dossair has its own system; do not apply Rizqey Labs colours blindly.

**Dossair is the star in anything Faris sends out; use the Dossair look.**

- Light canvas: paper `#F5F6F3`; cards `#FFFFFF`; ink `#10191E`; graphite `#56626A`; rules `#DADFDA`.
- Accent: registry teal `#0E5A61`, with tint `#E2EEEC`.
- DARK is the default look: canvas `#0B1114`, ink `#E7ECEA`, muted text `#98A4A9`, hairline rules `#26323A`, and mint-teal accent `#6CC0BC`. Light is an optional toggle only.
- Display: Newsreader with italic accents; body: Inter; labels: IBM Plex Mono.
- It is also editorial, with generous paper-like space, monospaced labels, serif statements, restrained teal emphasis and fine rules.

## Voice

Plain, direct, specific, and accountable. State what the product does and where human review happens. Avoid inflated claims, jargon, filler, and em dashes.

## QUALITY STANDARD

A deck is good enough for a corporate event organiser only when every slide has one idea, big readable type, a real Rizqey logo, no clutter, no jargon, no em dashes, and strong contrast. All text must remain editable. Check the rendered slides for cut-off text, collisions, weak contrast, distorted logos, uneven spacing, and off-brand colour or typography before delivery.
